Noor Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Noor (“the app”) is a prayer times, Qur’an and Qibla app made by Tarik at Cykie Labs. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information.

The short version

Noor has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your prayer log, duas, bookmarks, settings and progress stay on your phone. Noor never sells or shares your data.

What stays on your device

Everything you create or choose in Noor is stored only on your device: your location or chosen city, calculation method, prayer journal, My Duas, bookmarks and reading position, Khatm plan, Jannah Garden, Learn Arabic progress, downloaded Qur’an text and recitations, language and theme. It is never uploaded to the developer.

Your location

Prayer times and the Qibla direction are calculated on your phone from your location (or the city you pick). Your location is not sent anywhere for this. Only if you open Nearby mosques does Noor send your approximate coordinates to the public OpenStreetMap Overpass service to find mosques around you; that request contains no name, account or device identifier.

Content Noor downloads

To show content you ask for, Noor downloads it from these services. The requests contain only what is needed — for example a surah number, a language or a reciter — and nothing that identifies you:

  • Qur’an text and public-domain translations from api.alquran.cloud (Tanzil text), with a copy on jsDelivr as a backup;
  • recitations and the Urdu translation voice from EveryAyah (everyayah.com);
  • additional translations and word-by-word meanings from the Quran Foundation, through a small Cykie Labs server on Cloudflare that passes the request on. It stores no personal data; Cloudflare keeps standard short-lived technical logs (such as the time and the address of a request) to run the service;
  • hadith collections from jsDelivr.

Like any website, these services can see your device’s IP address when it connects to them. Downloaded surahs and recitations are kept on your phone so they work offline; translations from the Quran Foundation are refreshed at least once a week, as their terms require.

Notifications

Adhan, reminder and Jumu‘ah notifications are scheduled and delivered by your phone’s own notification system. Nothing about them leaves your device.

Permissions

  • Location — optional; for prayer times and Qibla. You can choose a city instead.
  • Notifications — optional; for the adhan and reminders.
  • Photos (add only) — only when you save a wallpaper or share card.

Text-to-speech, the compass and home-screen widgets all run on your device.

Purchases and donations

Noor Plus and tips are processed entirely by Apple (or Google Play on Android). Noor never sees your payment details; it only learns from the store whether a purchase is active.

Your control

  • Export a backup any time from More › Settings › Your data.
  • Delete all data any time from More › Settings › Your data › Reset.
  • Uninstalling Noor removes all of its data from your device.

Children

Noor is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here and the date above will change.
