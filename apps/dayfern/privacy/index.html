Privacy policy

Dayfern privacy policy

Dayfern is an iPhone app by Cykie Labs, made by Tarik. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use it.

Effective 17 November 2026

The short version

  • Dayfern does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • Your tasks, routines, habits, moods, journal, energy check-ins and settings stay on your iPhone.

Who is responsible

Cykie Labs is the developer of Dayfern and the data controller for this policy. You can reach us at hello@cykielabs.com. Because the app does not collect personal data, we have no personal data about you from Dayfern.

What stays on your device

Dayfern keeps the following only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage:

  • your tasks, events, routines, habits, templates and countdowns;
  • your mood check-ins, energy check-ins, journal entries, gratitude notes and any photos you add to the journal;
  • your focus sessions, garden progress, theme and other settings;
  • automatic backups, which are also kept inside the app on your phone.

We never receive this information. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. If you export a backup yourself, the file goes wherever you choose to save or share it.

Permissions

  • Notifications. Reminders and the 5-minute heads-up are scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server.
  • Calendars. If you choose to show your phone's calendars, Dayfern reads those events on your device to display them. It does not change or upload them.
  • Photos. Dayfern only sees the photos you pick yourself for a journal entry.

You can change these at any time in your iPhone's Settings, under Dayfern.

Fonts

The first time a font style is used, Dayfern downloads the font file from Google Fonts and keeps it on your phone. Like any request over the internet, Google can see your device's IP address for that download. No personal information or app data is sent with it.

What we don't do

  • We don't create accounts or ask who you are.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs for analytics or ads.
  • We don't sell, rent or share personal data, because we don't collect any.

Crash reports from Apple

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and app usage statistics. These are collected and anonymised by Apple, do not identify you, and are used only to fix problems. You can turn this off at any time in the same place.

Children

Dayfern is made for a general audience. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Health information

Dayfern is a planning tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat ADHD or any other condition.

Your rights

Data protection laws give you rights over your personal data, such as access and deletion. Dayfern doesn't send us any personal data, so there is nothing for us to access or delete. Everything the app keeps is on your device, where you can delete it by deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll publish the new version at this address and update the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about Dayfern? Email hello@cykielabs.com.
