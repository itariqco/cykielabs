How a project goes

Small steps, no surprises.

You'll always know what's being built, what it costs and when you can try it.

  1. A conversation

    Tell us what you want to make. We ask questions, point out risks and say plainly if we're not the right fit.

  2. A clear plan and quote

    A written scope, a timeline and a fixed price or a simple monthly rate. No vague estimates.

  3. Builds you can try

    Regular test builds on your own phone through TestFlight and Google Play testing.

  4. Launch

    We prepare the store listing and see the app through review on both stores.

  5. Looking after it

    Optional ongoing care. You own the code and the store accounts either way.

Tell us about your idea.

A few lines is enough to start: what the app should do, who it's for and when you'd like it.
