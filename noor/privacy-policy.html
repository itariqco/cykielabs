Nur al-Ummah Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Nur al-Ummah (“the app”) is a prayer times, Qur’an and Qibla app made by Tarik at Cykie Labs. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information.

The short version

Nur al-Ummah has no account, no ads and no analytics. Your prayer log, duas, bookmarks, settings and progress stay on your phone, with a backup copy in your own iCloud or Google account. Nur al-Ummah never sells or shares your data.

What stays on your device

Everything you create or choose in Nur al-Ummah is stored on your device: your location or chosen city, calculation method, prayer journal, My Duas, bookmarks and reading position, Khatm plan, Jannah Garden, Learn Arabic progress, downloaded Qur’an text and recitations, language and theme. It is never uploaded to the developer.

Backup of your data

So nothing is lost when you reinstall Nur al-Ummah or change phones, your settings, prayer journal, bookmarks and duas are also kept in your own cloud account: your iCloud on iPhone (Apple’s key-value storage) and Google Auto Backup on Android when backups are turned on. Only you can access these copies; Cykie Labs cannot read them. Downloaded recitations are not included. You can also save a backup file yourself and restore it later.

Your location

Prayer times and the Qibla direction are calculated on your phone from your location (or the city you pick). Your location is not sent anywhere for this. Only if you open Nearby mosques: on iPhone, the app asks Apple Maps for mosques around your saved location; on Android, the button opens Google Maps, which then sees that location. The app sends no name, account or device identifier.

Content the app downloads

The Arabic Qur’an text (Tanzil Project, CC BY 3.0) is built into the app. When you ask for content, the app downloads it from these services. The requests contain only what is needed (for example a surah number, a language or a reciter) and nothing that identifies you:

  • translations of the meanings of the Qur’an from QuranEnc.com;
  • recitations, recitations with translation and word timings from Quran Foundation (verses.quran.com, quranicaudio.com, api.quran.com);
  • word-by-word meanings from Quran Foundation, through a small Cykie Labs server on Cloudflare that passes the request on. It stores no personal data; Cloudflare keeps standard short-lived technical logs (such as the time and the address of a request) to run the service;
  • hadith, translations and explanations from HadeethEnc.com.

Like any website, these services can see your device’s IP address when it connects to them. Translations and hadith you open are kept on your phone so they work offline and are refreshed when their publishers update them; Quran Foundation data is refreshed at least once a week, as its terms require. Recitation is streamed.

Notifications

Adhan, reminder and Jumu‘ah notifications are scheduled and delivered by your phone’s own notification system. Nothing about them leaves your device.

Permissions

  • Location: optional; for prayer times, the Qibla and nearby mosques. You can choose a city instead.
  • Notifications: optional; for the adhan, reminders and the Qur’an player.
  • Photos (add only): only when you save a wallpaper or share card.

Text-to-speech, the compass and home-screen widgets all run on your device.

Purchases and donations

The app has no purchases at the moment. If optional purchases are offered later, they will be processed entirely by Apple (or Google Play on Android), and the app will never see your payment details.

Your control

  • Export or restore a backup any time from More › Settings › Your data.
  • Delete all data any time from More › Settings › Your data › Reset. This also clears the iCloud copy.
  • Uninstalling Nur al-Ummah removes its data from your device. A cloud backup stays in your own account until you delete it in your phone’s settings (iCloud › Manage storage, or Google › Backup).

Children

Nur al-Ummah is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here and the date above will change.
