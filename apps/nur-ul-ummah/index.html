Prayer & Qur'an

Nur ul Ummah

A calm companion for your salah, your Qur'an and your day.

Accurate prayer times under a living sky, the adhan right on time, and the full Qur'an to read and listen to, offline. No account and no ads. Your journal, duas and settings stay on your phone.

App Store

Features

What's inside.

Prayer times and adhan

11 calculation methods, Hanafi or standard Asr, six adhan voices and gentle reminders before a prayer ends.

The Qur'an, read and heard

The full Uthmani text offline, meanings in 25+ languages, and words that light up as the reciter reads.

Qibla and mosques

A precise Qibla compass, a sun method when there's no compass, and mosques near you on Apple Maps.

Learn and grow

Wudu and salah step by step, Islam basics, Learn Arabic lessons and hadith with explanations.

Duas and dhikr

Morning and evening adhkar with sources, a tasbih counter, a prayer journal and the Jannah Garden.

Beautiful and personal

Living Islamic wallpapers, share cards, home screen widgets and full right to left support.

Privacy

No account, no ads and no tracking. Your journal, duas and settings stay on your phone, with a backup in your own iCloud.
