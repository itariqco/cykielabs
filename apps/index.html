Period & cycle trackerCykie

A period tracker that feels more like a notebook than a medical chart. Log a day in two taps, see when your next period and fertile days are due, and dress the whole app up the way you like. Everything you log stays on your phone.

Live wallpapersSkyshift

Wallpapers that keep time with you, drawn live in code. Mornings look like morning, sunsets turn gold, and after dark the stars, city windows and northern lights come on. Every scene is drawn in code, so there are no image packs to download.
