Common questions

Quick answers.

Didn't find it here? Email us and we'll sort it out.

A product isn't found. What can I do?

Groclens looks products up in community-run databases, so some items aren't in them yet. You can add the product yourself at Open Food Facts so everyone can see it, or use the label reader to take a photo of the label and read it with the camera.

Some product information looks wrong.

Product details come from community and public databases such as Open Food Facts, Wikidata and openFDA, not from us. If something is wrong, you can report or edit it at the source, and the fix will show in Groclens too. If you think Groclens itself is showing it wrongly, email us.

The camera isn't working.

Groclens needs camera access to scan. On your iPhone open Settings, then Groclens, and turn on Camera. If it's already on, close the app fully and open it again. You can also type the barcode or pick a photo instead.

Does Groclens work offline?

Your scan history and favourites are saved on your phone, so you can open them without a connection. Looking up a new product needs the internet, because the information comes from online databases.

How do I clear my history?

Open Settings in Groclens and clear your history there. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored on your phone.

Can I rely on the allergy warnings?

They're a helpful check, not a guarantee. Warnings depend on the ingredient and allergen data in the databases, which may be incomplete or out of date. Always read the product's packaging, and ask your doctor if you're unsure. Groclens isn't medical or nutritional advice.

Does Groclens collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. To look a product up the app sends only the barcode or search words to public databases. The full details are in the privacy policy.

How do I contact you?

Email hello@cykielabs.com. For a bug, tell us your iPhone model, iOS version, the Groclens version and what happened, and include the barcode if it's about a product. A screenshot helps a lot.

Write to hello@cykielabs.com and tell us it's about Groclens. We'll get back to you.
