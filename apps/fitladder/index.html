Workout planner

FitLadder

A strength coach that tells you what to do today, then moves you up as you get stronger.

Follow a plan built for your goal, log every set and see your progress in clear, honest numbers. Your workouts, measurements and photos stay on your phone, and the whole app works offline.

Features

What's inside.

Plans that progress

Home, dumbbell or gym plans from beginner to expert. The player suggests your next reps or weight from last time.

Skill ladders

Five bodyweight progressions, from wall push ups to archer push ups and squats to pistol squats, with a test on every rung.

150 animated moves

An animated coach shows every exercise with steps, tips, common mistakes and easier or harder variations.

Track every lift

A workout log with records, a strength score from 0 to 1000, a recovery map and monthly recaps.

Coaching extras

Voice coach, tempo coach, smart rest timer on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, and a plate calculator.

FitLadder Pro

An optional subscription with a plan generator, lift forecasts, coach reports and an Apple Watch app.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Workouts, measurements and progress photos stay on your phone.
