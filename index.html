About

A small studio, on purpose.

Cykie Labs is run by Tarik. Every app is designed, built and supported in house, so the person answering your email is the one who wrote the code.

We'd rather make a handful of apps people keep for years than chase downloads, and we bring the same care to client work: clear plans, regular builds you can try, and no surprises at the end.
