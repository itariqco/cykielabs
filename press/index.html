About Cykie Labs

Cykie Labs is an independent app studio run by Tarik. It makes private, offline iPhone apps (FitLadder, Groclens, Cykie, Skyshift and Resume Builder) and builds apps for clients.

Our apps need no account, show no ads and keep what people enter on their own device.
