ADHD-friendly day planner

Dayfern

A calm, visual day planner that plans around your energy.

Brain-dump everything on your mind and Dayfern turns it into a plan. It puts heavy work in your fresh morning hours, tells you gently when a day is too full, and shows just one thing when it all feels like too much. Made for busy minds, and designed with ADHD in mind.

Features

What's inside.

Brain-dump

Type everything on your mind in plain words. Dayfern turns it into tasks and finds the free time.

Plan around your energy

Check in each morning. Heavy work goes in your best hours, rest after lunch, and you see when a day is too full.

Lighten my day

One tap moves the least important tasks to tomorrow, with Undo.

Just one thing

A calm screen with only the task to do now and one big Done button.

Too much?

Shrink your list to the three things that matter most. The rest can wait.

Day Dial & timeline

Your whole day as a 24-hour clock, plus a visual timeline with the free time shown.

Focus timer

A visual timer with Pomodoro and a mixer of calming sounds like rain, waves and café.

Routines & habits

Step-by-step routines, habits with streaks and a gentle heads-up 5 minutes before each task.

Reflect & grow

Mood check-ins, a journal and gratitude jar. Every task you finish grows a little garden.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your plans, moods and journal stay on your iPhone. Dayfern has no servers and never sees your data.
