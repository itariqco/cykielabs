Groclens privacy policy

Groclens is an iPhone app by Cykie Labs, made by Tarik. This policy explains, in plain language, what happens to your information when you use it.

Effective 15 November 2026

The short version

  • Groclens does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal data.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • Your scan history, favourites, allergy choices and theme stay on your iPhone.
  • To look a product up, the app sends only the barcode you scanned or the words you typed to public product databases. Nothing about you goes with it.

Who is responsible

Cykie Labs is the developer of Groclens and the data controller for this policy. You can reach us at hello@cykielabs.com. Because the app does not collect personal data, we have no personal data about you from Groclens.

What stays on your device

Groclens keeps the following only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage:

  • your scan history and favourites, so you can see them again, including offline;
  • the allergens you choose, so the app can warn you about them;
  • your theme and other settings.

We never receive this information. You can clear your history at any time in the app's Settings, and deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Camera and photos

  • Camera. The camera is used only to read barcodes and QR codes while the scan screen is open, and to take a photo of a label when you choose to.
  • Label photos. Label photos are read on your device using Apple's Vision text recognition. They are never uploaded, and the app does not save them.
  • Photo library. Groclens only sees the photos you pick yourself, to read a barcode or label from them.

You can change camera and photo access at any time in your iPhone's Settings, under Groclens.

What the app sends to look products up

To show you information about a product, Groclens sends the scanned barcode, or the search words you typed, directly from your iPhone to the public services below. No name, account, location, history, allergy choices or other personal information is sent, and nothing passes through a Cykie Labs server.

  • Open Food Facts, Open Beauty Facts, Open Pet Food Facts and Open Products Facts, run by the Open Food Facts association: food, cosmetics, pet food and other product details, scores, ingredients and images.
  • Open Library, run by the Internet Archive: book details for ISBN barcodes.
  • openFDA, run by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration: US recall notices and US medicine information.
  • Wikidata and Wikipedia, run by the Wikimedia Foundation: company country, ownership and history.
  • UPCitemdb: product names, as a fallback when the other databases don't know a barcode.

These are independent services, each with its own privacy policy, which you can find on their websites. Like any request over the internet, they can see your device's IP address when the app contacts them. We don't control and don't receive any data they keep.

What we don't do

  • We don't create accounts or ask who you are.
  • We don't show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs for analytics or ads.
  • We don't sell, rent or share personal data, because we don't collect any.

Crash reports from Apple

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and app usage statistics. These are collected and anonymised by Apple, do not identify you, and are used only to fix problems. You can turn this off at any time in the same place.

Children

Groclens is made for a general audience. It doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Data protection laws give you rights over your personal data, such as access and deletion. Groclens doesn't send us any personal data, so there is nothing for us to access or delete. Everything the app keeps is on your device, where you can clear it in Settings or by deleting the app. For anything kept by one of the services listed above, please contact that service directly.

Health information

Groclens is for information only and is not medical or nutritional advice. Product data comes from community and public databases and can be missing, out of date or wrong. Allergy warnings may be incomplete, so always read the product's packaging. Medicine and recall information applies to products sold in the United States.

Data sources and licences

Open Food Facts data is available under the Open Database License (ODbL), and its product images under Creative Commons BY-SA. Wikipedia text is available under Creative Commons BY-SA 4.0. Wikidata data is available under CC0.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll publish the new version at this address and update the effective date above.
