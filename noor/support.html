Common questions

Prayer times look different from my mosque

Open More › Settings › Prayer and choose the calculation method your mosque uses, and Standard or Hanafi for Asr. Make sure your location is right (tap your city at the top of Settings).

The adhan didn’t play

Allow notifications for Nur ul Ummah in your iPhone Settings, keep Adhan at prayer times on in More › Settings › Adhan & reminders, then tap Test the adhan in 1 minute. With the ring/silent switch on silent, iPhone plays notifications without sound.

The Qibla compass seems wrong

Move away from metal and magnets and wave your phone in a figure-of-eight to calibrate. The Sun Qibla card on the same screen tells you times when the sun points to the Kaaba, with no compass needed.

How do I use the Qur’an offline?

In Library › Qur’an, tap Options on the “Use the Qur’an offline” card. Choose Download everything, Text only, or tap the download icon next to individual surahs.

How do I hear the translation after each ayah?

In the reader, swipe the row under “Arabic | With meaning” and turn on Hear meaning. Urdu uses the recorded voice of Shamshad Ali Khan; other languages use your phone’s voices (add more in iPhone Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices).

How do I change the app’s language?

More › Settings › Language. Nur ul Ummah is available in 27 languages, and Urdu, Arabic, Persian and Pashto display right-to-left.

How do I set a wallpaper that changes from day to night?

Open a scene in Wallpapers and tap the sunrise button, then follow the Shortcuts steps shown in the app (a Personal Automation using “Get Nur ul Ummah Wallpaper for Now” and “Set Wallpaper”).
