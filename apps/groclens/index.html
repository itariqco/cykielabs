Barcode & product scanner

Groclens

Scan a barcode and see what's really in it, where it's made and who owns it.

Point your iPhone at a barcode or QR code and Groclens shows the scores, nutrition, ingredients, allergens and additives, then goes further: the country behind the brand, the company that owns it and any US FDA recalls.

Features

What's inside.

Scan anything

Point the camera at a barcode or QR code, type a barcode, or pick a photo.

Scores at a glance

Nutri-Score, NOVA and Green-Score, plus nutrition like calories and protein.

Allergy warnings

Choose your allergens and Groclens flags them in ingredients, along with additives.

Who's behind it

Where a product is made, which country the company is from, its ownership chain and history.

Recall checks

Checks US FDA recalls, and also looks up books by ISBN and US medicines.

Read the label

Snap a photo of a label and Groclens reads it on your device.

History & favourites

Everything you scan is saved on your phone and works offline.

Themes

Pick the look you like.

Free and private

No ads, no account, no in-app purchases and no tracking.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your history, favourites and allergy choices stay on your iPhone. To look a product up, Groclens sends only the barcode or the words you typed to public product databases.
